We build Solitaire, Sudoku and Tetris at PlayStudios — mobile games with tens of millions of downloads. Now we're building on Roblox.

Survive the Horde is a co-op survivors-like. Up to four players hold off waves of skeletons and goblins, level up weapons between fights, and take on fortress bosses.

Roblox Jumpstart

The game was selected for the Roblox Jumpstart program, which provides resources and consultation from the Roblox team to support a game's launch and curated placement on Roblox Home through the Standout Games sort.
